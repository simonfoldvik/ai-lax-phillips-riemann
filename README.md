# AI Experiments on the Lax–Phillips–Faddeev–Pavlov–Riemann Asymptotics

A collection of experiments pointing AI at the Lax–Phillips scattering asymptotics in the Faddeev–Pavlov setting.

Proving the Lax–Phillips bound is known to be equivalent to proving the Riemann hypothesis (see Lax' *Functional Analysis*).

Every `.tex` file and all mathematics in this repo was generated by AI (OpenAI Codex 5.6 Sol Ultra) and is shared as is. The author of this repo does not certify the mathematical contents are correct or novel. The generated references may or may not exist.

## Rationale

Although it would be a far shot for AI to settle this (it is RH-hard), it is at the same time akin to the kind of formulation which AI systems have lately been demonstrating a capacity to solve.

The OpenAI counterexample to the [Erdös unit distance problem](https://openai.com/index/model-disproves-discrete-geometry-conjecture/) in May 2026 (see [arXiv:2605.20695](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20695) for expert commentary) gives a negative resolution of a long-presumed asymptotics and *shares properties with the Lax–Phillips problem* (i.e., they both ask for a proof or a counterexample to a presumed asymptotic rate).

It is therefore interesting to investigate whether AI systems can find a proof or counterexample through the Lax–Phillips–Faddeev–Pavlov connection.
